    About the model

    xG is a logistic function of shot distance, the angle between the posts, and sideways offset, with adjustments for body part, how the chance arises, defenders in the lane, pressure and goalkeeper position. The coefficients are set by hand so common spots land near typical open-play values (about 0.15 from the penalty spot area, about 0.06 from 20 m, close to 0.45 in the six-yard box). A penalty kick is fixed at 0.78. It is a teaching model and is not fitted to any data provider's shots.
